Late Knight RTS
Account deletion

Developer / publisher: UnYawn

To request deletion of your Late Knight RTS account and associated personal data, email our monitored support inbox. You can make a request without the app installed. You do not need to know your PlayFab player ID.

Start an email request

Email: unyawndeveloper@gmail.com

This button opens a draft in your email app. You must send the email yourself. This page does not submit a request. If no email app opens, copy the address into your usual email service.

What to include

  1. Use the subject Late Knight RTS account deletion request and say that you want your account and associated data deleted.
  2. Send the request from the email address associated with your game account, if you have one, and include your in-game name if known. We will check the email against our account records and verify ownership before deleting anything.
  3. For a device-linked guest profile, an email address may not be linked to the game. In app versions with Request deletion, choose that account-menu button, then Copy & open and paste the supplied details into your email. The app supplies the player ID and a verification code automatically; you do not need to find or type the ID. The code is valid for 24 hours; generating another replaces the previous code.
  4. If you no longer have the app, cannot sign in, or cannot copy those details, email us anyway. We will discuss another way to locate the account and verify ownership. An incoming email address, in-game name or player ID alone is not proof of ownership.

Share verification codes only with this support inbox. Never send passwords, sign-in tokens, session tickets, raw device identifiers or payment-card details.

What happens next

We review your email, verify that you control the account, and confirm the account and data covered by your request before processing it. We may ask for additional evidence or a new code. We will explain the expected processing time when we respond and email you when processing is complete, including any remaining data or limitations.

Deletion is permanent and can remove access to saved progress, scores, inventory and virtual currency. Do not assume they can be restored. A request does not automatically cancel an app-store subscription or arrange a refund; manage any applicable purchases with the store.

Data covered by your request

We review the Late Knight RTS PlayFab account and associated data, including profile information, linked sign-in information, saved progress, statistics, inventory and virtual currency. Some PlayFab account information may be shared across games published by UnYawn. We will explain any broader effect and obtain your confirmation before deleting a shared publisher account.

GameAnalytics, Meta/Facebook, Photon and app-store services may process data separately. Deleting a PlayFab account does not automatically erase those services' records or delete your Apple, Google or Facebook account. We review data we can identify and control, handle applicable provider requests, and explain any separate steps or limitations.

Retention and exceptions

Some records may need to be retained for legal obligations, fraud prevention, security or resolving disputes; backups and provider logs may follow their own retention schedules. If this applies, we will identify the retained data, reason and applicable retention period in our response. We do not promise immediate removal of every record from every provider.

We use your request and verification information to verify ownership and handle the request. The app's verification code expires after 24 hours; expiry prevents its use as proof and does not itself erase the stored verification record. Ask us about any remaining support correspondence or verification records when confirming your request.
